Subscription FAQ

Q: How can I upgrade my subscription?
A: Navigate to Account Settings and select Upgrade Plan.

Q: Can I downgrade my plan?
A: Yes, changes take effect at the next billing cycle.

Q: Can I cancel my subscription?
A: Yes, users may cancel at any time through Account Settings.

Q: Will my data be deleted after cancellation?
A: No, account data remains available for 30 days.
